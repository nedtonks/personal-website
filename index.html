About me

I am a Computer Science student at Swinburne University, completing co-minors in Finance and FinTech. I have a learning-first mindset and enjoy breaking down complex problems, improving systems and processes, and understanding how things work at a deeper level.

I recently completed a Software Engineer internship at Maurice Blackburn Lawyers, where, using agentic workflows, I built a secure portal for external organisations from the ground up and improved the firm’s AI chat assistant and its accompanying internal admin dashboard. I am now an Assurance Vacationer in Risk & Controls Assurance at PwC.

I am drawn to problem domains shaped by real-world constraints, particularly the intersection of finance and technology. Before moving into tech, I worked as a bookkeeper and across retail and warehousing, which built the attention to detail, accountability and teamwork I bring to every project.

Outside of tech, I like to stay active and busy. I enjoy spending time with friends, playing golf, running and going to the gym.
